Rules and how to play

How to play:

  1. Enter your name and press Start game. You begin with $1000.
  2. Click the chips to place your bet, then press Deal Hand.
  3. Press Hit to take another card or Stay to keep your hand. With exactly 21 your turn ends automatically.
  4. After you stay, the dealer plays and the result is shown.
  5. Your name and balance are saved in your browser, so you can continue later.

Scoring:

  • Ace = 1 or 11 (whichever is better for your hand)
  • Face Cards (J, Q, K) = 10
  • Number Cards = Value of card

Bets:

  • Bets are placed only before the cards are dealt
  • You cannot bet more than your balance
  • Clear Bet removes your chips before dealing
  • No double down, no split, no side bets, no insurance

Dealer:

  • Dealer shows one card at the start
  • Dealer must hit until reaching 17 or higher
  • If you bust, the dealer does not draw more cards

Who wins:

  • You win if your sum is bigger than the dealer's without busting
  • You win if the dealer busts and you did not bust
  • You lose if you bust or the dealer's sum is bigger
  • Same sum as the dealer = tie

Payout:

  • Win: you get your bet back plus the same amount
  • Tie: your bet is returned
  • Loss: you lose your bet
  • No bonus for a blackjack
  • If your balance reaches $0, it resets to $1000
